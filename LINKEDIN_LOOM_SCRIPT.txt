NARRATION (final)
-----------------
We built Jobform as production AI refund support for commerce — paired with NovaShop, a real storefront.

Customers shop and track orders in NovaShop. When they need help, Jobform opens support locked to that customer and that order.

The agent runs the conversation — chat or microphone. A deterministic policy engine decides eligibility and amount. The ledger re-validates before anything is written.

Ops teams see the same truth in the staff console: conversations, the refund ledger, published policy checklists, and store integrations.

Policy decides the money. The agent handles the conversation.
