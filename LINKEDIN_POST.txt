We built Jobform — AI customer support for e-commerce refunds where the model never gets to decide the money.

The usual failure mode with “AI support” is simple: the chatbot sounds confident and invents an approval. Jobform flips that. The agent runs the conversation (text or voice). A deterministic policy engine decides eligibility and amount. An idempotent ledger writes the refund. Staff can escalate, approve edge cases, and inspect the full run.

What’s real in the demo:
• Order-locked support sessions (portal lookup or store-launched handoff)
• Tool-using agent with live policy checks
• Refund execution that re-validates inside a transaction
• Voice on the same path as typed chat
• Staff console for conversations, escalations, refunds, and policy
