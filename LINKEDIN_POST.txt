I wanted to build an AI customer support agent that works with a real commerce experience — not just another chatbot running in isolation.

So I integrated it with NovaShop, a sample storefront with real products, customers, and orders. The AI doesn’t need to own the shop. It connects to the business data that already exists.

When a customer opens an order, the system already knows status, payment, tracking, and items — and they can get help without retyping everything. That context flows into Jobform: the AI support agent.

The agent can talk in text or voice. But it doesn’t invent refunds. Money decisions go through deterministic business policy first.

Staff still keep control — conversations, customers, escalations, and a refund ledger that makes every decision traceable.
