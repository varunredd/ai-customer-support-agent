We built Jobform — production AI refund support for commerce, paired with NovaShop as the live storefront.

The usual AI-support failure mode is simple: the chatbot sounds confident and invents an approval. Jobform flips that.

Customers shop and track orders in NovaShop. When they need help, Jobform opens support locked to that customer and that order. The agent runs the conversation — chat or microphone. A deterministic policy engine decides eligibility and amount. The ledger re-validates before anything is written.

Ops teams work from the same system of record: conversations, refund ledger, published policy checklists, and store integrations. The model never gets financial authority.
