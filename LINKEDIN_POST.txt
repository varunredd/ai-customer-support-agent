We just shipped Jobform — AI refund support built for real commerce operations.

Most “AI support” demos let the model invent approvals. Jobform does the opposite.

Customers shop and track orders in NovaShop. When they need help, Jobform opens support locked to that customer and order. The agent runs the conversation — chat or voice. A deterministic policy engine decides eligibility and amount. The ledger re-validates before anything is written.

Staff get the full ops surface: conversations, refunds ledger, published policy checklists, and store integrations. The model never gets financial authority.
