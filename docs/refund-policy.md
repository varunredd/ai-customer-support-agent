# Strict Refund Policy

**Version:** 2026-08-18  
**Applies to:** automated e-commerce refunds handled by the AI Customer Support Agent.

1. The customer account must be active. Suspended accounts cannot receive automated refunds.
2. Accounts classified as **HIGH risk** are denied by the automated refund path.
3. The requesting customer must own the order.
4. Only delivered orders can be refunded.
5. Requests must be submitted within **30 calendar days of delivery**.
6. An item marked non-refundable is not eligible under any reason.
7. **Final-sale items are never refundable.**
8. Refund quantity must be at least one and cannot exceed the remaining unrefunded purchased quantity.
9. For changed-mind, late-delivery, or not-as-described requests, the item must be **unopened**.
10. For damaged or wrong-item claims, the item may be opened/damaged but must not have been consumed or used.
11. Automated refunds reimburse the eligible item price only. Shipping is not included.
12. Cumulative refunds may never exceed the order's paid amount.

The LLM may explain these rules, but it may **not invent exceptions, override a failed rule, or calculate eligibility without calling the policy validation tool**.
